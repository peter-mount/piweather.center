\sectionpaged{Heat Index}

The heat index\index{Heat Index} is an index that combines air temperature and relative humidity, in shaded areas,
and represents how a human perceives the temperature.
It is only valid for temperatures above $80^{\circ}F$.

Heat Index is defined by the following Formula:

$ T_{hi} = 61 + T_a + 1.2(T_a - 68) + 0.095\,R_h $

If $T_{hi} \geq 80^{\circ}F$ then calculate it using Rothfusz regression:

$ T_{hi} = \begin{array}[t]{l}
    42.379 \\
    + 2.04901523 \, T_a \\
    + 10.14333127 \, R_h \\
    - 0.22475541 \, T_a \, R_h \\
    - 6.83783x10^{-3} \, {T_a}^2 \\
    - 5.481717x10^{-2} \, {R_h}^2 \\
    + 1.22874x10^{-3} \, {T_a}^2 \, R_h \\
    + 8.5282x10^{-4} \, T_a \, {R_h}^2 \\
    - 1.99x10^{-6} \, {T_a}^2 \, {R_h}^2
    \end{array}
$

where:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3em,style=nextline]
\item[$T_{hi}$] the \texttt{Heat Index}, based on the Fahrenheit scale
\item[$T_a$] the \texttt{Ambient temperature}\index{Temperature, Ambient} in degrees Fahrenheit
\item[$R_h$] the \texttt{Relative Humidity}\index{Relative Humidity}
\end{description}
