\sectionpaged{Feels Like}
\index{Feels Like}
\index{Temperature!Feels Like}

Feels Like is the temperature a human perceives based on the actual temperature, relative humidity and wind speed.
It is a composite function based on either \GoFunctionRef{WindChill} or \GoFunctionRef{HeatIndex} dependent on the prevailing
weather conditions.

\begin{definition}Feels Like:
\begin{eqnarray*}
    \FeelsLike &=&
    \begin{cases}
        \GoFunctionCall{WindChill}{\AmbientTemperature, \WindSpeed} & \quad \textbf{if } \AmbientTemperature\leq\Fahrenheit{50} \land \WindSpeed\geq\MilesPerHour{3}\\
        \GoFunction{HeatIndex}( \AmbientTemperature, \RelativeHumidity) & \quad \textbf{if } \AmbientTemperature\geq\Fahrenheit{80} \\
        \AmbientTemperature & \quad \textbf{otherwise}
    \end{cases}
\end{eqnarray*}

\WhereDescription{
    \item[$\FeelsLike$]             The feels like temperature in \Fahrenheit{}
    \item[$\AmbientTemperature$]    the air temperature in \Fahrenheit{}
    \item[$\WindSpeed$]             the wind speed in \MilesPerHour{}
    \item[$\RelativeHumidity$]      Relative Humidity
}
\end{definition}
