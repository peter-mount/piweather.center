\subsection{Feels Like}

Feels Like\index{Feels Like} is the temperature a human perceives based on
the actual temperature, relative humidity and wind speed.

\[
    T_{fl} =
    \begin{cases}
        T_{wc} = \textbf{WindChill}( T_a, v_{wind} ) & \quad \textbf{if } T_a \leq 50^{\circ}F \and v_{wind} \leq 3 mph\\
        T_{hi} = \textbf{HeatIndex}( T_a, R_h) & \quad \textbf{if } T_a \geq 80^{\circ}F \\
        T_a & \quad \textbf{otherwise}
    \end{cases}
\]

where:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3em,style=nextline]
    \item[$T_{fl}$]     The Feels like temperature in degrees Fahrenheit
    \item[$T_a$]        the air temperature in degrees Fahrenheit
    \item[$v_{wind}$]   the wind speed in miles per hour
    \item[$R_h$]        Relative Humidity
\end{description}
