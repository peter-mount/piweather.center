\sectionpaged{Wind Chill}
\labsec{WindChill}
\index{Wind Chill}
\index{Temperature!Wind Chill}

Wind chill is the sensation of cold produced by
the wind for a given ambient air temperature on exposed skin.
It's values are always lower than the air temperature in the range where the
formula is valid.\sidesupercite{WikiWindChill}
It's only valid for temperatures at or below \Fahrenheit{50} and wind speeds above \MilesPerHour{3}.

\begin{definition}Wind Chill is defined by the following formula\sidesupercite{WeatherGovWindChill}:
\labdef{windChill}
\begin{eqnarray*}
\WindChill &=& 35.74 + 0.6215\,\AmbientTemperature -35.75\,\WindSpeed^{+0.16} + 0.4275\,\AmbientTemperature\,\WindSpeed^{+0.16}
\end{eqnarray*}

\WhereDescription{
    \item[\WindChill] the wind chill index in \Fahrenheit{}
    \item[\AmbientTemperature] the air temperature in \Fahrenheit{}
    \item[\WindSpeed] the wind speed in \MilesPerHour{}
}
\end{definition}
