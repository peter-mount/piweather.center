\sectionpaged{Wind Chill}
\index{Wind Chill}
\index{Temperature!Wind Chill}

Wind chill is the sensation of cold produced by
the wind for a given ambient air temperature on exposed skin.
It's values are always lower than the air temperature in the range where the
formula is valid.\sidesupercite{WikiWindChill}
It's only valid for temperatures at or below $50^{\circ}F$ and wind speeds above $3 mph$.

Wind Chill is defined by the following formula\sidesupercite{WeatherGovWindChill}:

\NewValueUnit{\WindChill}{T_{wc}}{Wind Chill Index}%
$ \WindChill = 35.74 + 0.6215\,\AmbientTemperature -35.75\,\WindSpeed^{+0.16} + 0.4275\,\AmbientTemperature\,\WindSpeed^{+0.16} $

where:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3em,style=nextline]
\item[$\WindChill$]   the wind chill index, based on the Fahrenheit scale
\item[$\AmbientTemperature$]      the air temperature in degrees Fahrenheit
\item[$\WindSpeed$] the wind speed in miles per hour
\end{description}
