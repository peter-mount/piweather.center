\subsection{Wind Chill}

Wind chill is the sensation of cold produced by
the wind for a given ambient air temperature on exposed skin.
It's values are always lower than the air temperature in the range where the
formula is valid.\sidecite{WikiWindChill,WeatherGovWindChill}
It's only valid for temperatures at or below $50^{\circ}F$ and wind speeds above $3 mph$.

Wind Chill is defined by the following formula\sidesupercite{WikiWindChill,WeatherGovWindChill}:

$T_{wc} = 35.74 + 0.6215\,T_a -35.75\,(v_{wind})^{+0.16} + 0.4275\,T_a\,(v_{wind})^{+0.16} $

where:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3em,style=nextline]
\item[$T_{wc}$]   the wind chill index, based on the Fahrenheit scale
\item[$T_a$]      the air temperature in degrees Fahrenheit
\item[$v_{wind}$] the wind speed in miles per hour
\end{description}
