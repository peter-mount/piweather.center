\section{Definitions}\label{sec:definitions}
For some values, especially temperature or velocity,
the unit will be determined by a specific formula.
Internally, the system will automatically convert into the required units.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=4em,style=nextline]
\item[$R_h$]        Relative Humidity as a percentage
\item[$T_a$]        Ambient temperature
\item[$T_{dp}$]     Dew point
\item[$T_{wc}$]     Wind Chill index
\item[$v_{wind}$]     wind speed.
\end{description}
